Quick Reference

Common Financial Terms Simplified

Click any financial term below to view its plain-English definition.

Inflation
The gradual increase in the general prices of goods and services over time, meaning your money buys fewer goods than before.
Interest Rate
The cost of borrowing money, set by lenders or central banks. Higher rates make loans and credit cards more expensive.
Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
The total market value of all finished goods and services produced within a country in a specific period, measuring economic health.
Federal Reserve (The Fed)
The central banking system of the United States, responsible for regulating monetary policy, controlling inflation, and stabilizing financial markets.
Treasury Yield
The interest rate that the government pays to investors who lend it money by purchasing government bonds.
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
A company's net profit divided by the number of outstanding shares, showing how much profit is allocated to each share of stock.
